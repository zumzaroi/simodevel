Who We Are and What We Want

SimoDevel is a non-profit learning initiative built by people passionate about electronics and mechatronics. We support learners from absolute beginner level to advanced design and simulation practice.

Our goal is simple: provide practical, technical, and free resources so anyone can build real skills - from first SPICE circuit to complete embedded projects. We value open collaboration, clear engineering documentation, and hands-on experimentation.
